Scrapjack

You built a flying suit from a car battery, two leaf blowers and a welding mask. It barely lifts you. Take it up, see what breaks, then make it better.

  • Every thruster is real. Hands, boots and back jet push from where they sit. Lose a boot and you’ll pull to one side.
  • Heat builds up. Push too hard and parts shut down mid-air.
  • Weight matters. Heavy armor or cargo can keep you on the ground.
  • Auto-steer. In flight the suit reads buildings and the ground ahead and corrects your course slightly. On Full, if that’s not enough, it hops a roof edge or rolls past at the last moment, keeping your speed. T switches Off / Light / Full.
  • Hover or fly. Press F to launch into nose-first flight with a burst of speed. Bank through the city, carve tight corners (A/D + S) and chase the drone.
  • Dodge missiles. East of the city, launchers fire at you. Roll to juke (Q / E), carve, hard-turn (double-tap), duck behind pillars or drop flares (Z).
